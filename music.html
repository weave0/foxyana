Album Notes

She learned rhythm from two sources: the Kolomyika meter in 2/4 time, and the way autumn wind moves through a Carpathian pass — unpredictable but never random. The tracks on this album live in the space between those two teachers.

Electronic music built from the same soil as the folk forms. The bass carries the weight of the steppe. The high frequencies remember the bandura. The silence between beats is the same silence between dance phrases — held, deliberate, earned.

Eight tracks. One album. A fox who learned to DJ because stillness, for her, is only the pause between frequencies.
